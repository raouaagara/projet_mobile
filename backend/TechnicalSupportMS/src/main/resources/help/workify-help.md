# Workify help guide

The support assistant answers only from this guide. Keep it true to the app: when a feature changes, change it here.

## The app
Workify connects clients who post jobs with freelancers who apply. The bottom bar has: Find work (freelancers) or
My projects (clients), Contracts, Messages, Community and Menu. The Menu holds My profile, Wallet, Help & support,
and for freelancers Skill tests & certifications, Subscription, Rewards, Referral and My availability; clients get
a World clock. Dark mode is in the Menu too.

## Accounts and profile
- Sign up as a client or as a freelancer. The role is chosen at sign-up.
- Edit your name, photo, cover, location, bio and links in My profile, then the pencil button. "Write with AI" under
  the bio writes it from your skills and a few notes; you can change it before saving.
- Skills: My profile, Skills, Edit. You can add them from the list or scan your CV (PDF, French or English): the AI
  finds your skills and you choose which ones to keep, then tap Save changes.
- Forgot password: use "Forgot password?" on the login screen.

## Finding work (freelancers)
- Jobs shows the open jobs. "Recommended for you" and the % match on each job come from the AI comparing your skills
  with each job. No skills on your profile means no score: add skills first.
- "Worth learning next" on your profile lists skills asked in jobs you almost fit; "Make my learning plan" gives
  an AI plan for them.
- To apply, open a job and tap Submit a proposal: your price, a delivery date and an optional cover letter
  ("Write with AI" drafts it). You can send one proposal per job. Follow the answers in My proposals.
- Save a job with the bookmark to find it in Saved.

## Posting a job (clients)
- My projects, then the + button. "Write your post with AI" turns a few words into a title, a description, a level
  and the skills; check it before publishing.
- Open your project, then View proposals: applicants are ranked by AI match, and you can read each cover letter.
  Accept or Decline. Accepting moves the project to "In progress" and offers to create the contract.

## Contracts
- The client creates the contract from an accepted proposal: start date, terms ("Write the terms with AI" can draft
  them), and signs it. The freelancer gets a notification, reads it ("Explain this contract" gives it in plain words),
  and signs or declines.
- Statuses: Sent (waiting for the freelancer), In progress, Completed, Declined, Cancelled.

## Payments, wallet and escrow
- Each user has a Wallet (Menu, Wallet). Clients add money there with Top up.
- When the freelancer signs, the contract amount is taken from the client's wallet and locked in escrow. The client
  needs enough money in the wallet before the freelancer can sign.
- When the work is done, the client taps "Approve & pay" on the contract: the escrow is released to the
  freelancer's wallet and the contract is completed. Then both can leave a review.
- Never pay or get paid outside Workify: outside payments are not protected by escrow, and the chat blocks
  messages that ask for them.

## Disputes
- On an active contract, "Report a problem" opens a dispute ("Make it clear with AI" helps write it). The money stays
  in escrow and a Workify admin reviews the case: they pay the freelancer, refund the client, or split it.
- Follow your disputes in Contracts, Disputes.

## Messages
- Message someone from their profile, a job or a contract. Messages are live, with read receipts, reactions,
  photos and files up to 10 MB.
- The ✨ button suggests replies. Messages with insults, threats, sexual content or requests to pay outside Workify
  are not sent, and you see why.
- Long-press a message to edit, delete, react or translate it. "Catch me up" summarizes a long conversation.

## Support
- Menu, Help & support. Ask the assistant first; if it can't solve it, open a request: the team answers in the app,
  usually within a day. Requests about money or fraud are treated first.

## Freelancer extras
- Skill tests & certifications: pass a test to show a certificate with your score on your profile.
- Subscription: plans unlock extras (such as the AI CV assistant); see Menu, Subscription for the current plans and
  prices.
- Referral: share your code; Rewards shows what you earned.
- My availability: your status and working hours, shown to clients in the chat.

## Community
- Groups, posts, clubs and events for clients and freelancers. Join a group to post in it.
- "Picked for you" (Community, Groups, Show me) suggests groups, clubs and upcoming events that fit your skills.
- In a group, "Write with AI" turns a few words into a clear post. Posts and comments with insults, threats,
  sexual content, scams or spam are not published, and you see why.

## Plans and referrals
- "Which plan fits me?" on the Subscription page looks at your activity and says which plan is worth it, or that
  staying free is fine for now.
- On the Referral page, "Write my invite with AI" writes a message with your code for WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email.
  A sign-up that looks suspicious is "Being checked": the bonus waits until an admin reviews it.
